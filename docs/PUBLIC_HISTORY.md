# Public-history notes (curated narratives)

These notes explain **why** GM Brasil cars are often discussed together.
They are **not** interchange certificates and do **not** invent OEM numbers.

## Família II (narrative)

Public automotive history commonly groups Monza → Vectra → Astra/Zafira era
around Family II engines and Opel-derived platforms in Brazil.

Useful starting points (verify yourself; pages change):

- [Chevrolet Vectra (pt.wikipedia)](https://pt.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chevrolet_Vectra)
- [Motor1 — último Vectra no Brasil](https://motor1.uol.com.br/features/777248/historia-ultimo-chevrolet-vectra-brasil/)

**Open Parts demo:** `SYN-FAMILY2-OIL-01` lists multiple Família II fixtures as
*educational applications* (synthetic SKUs only).

## Família I (narrative)

Workshop literature often discusses Corsa / Celta / Prisma / Meriva sharing
many service items on 1.0–1.8 Family I engines — always re-check year,
injection (EFI vs MPFI), and the stamped OEM code.

**Open Parts demo:** `SYN-FAMILY1-THERMO-01`, `SYN-FAMILY1-COIL-01`.

## Ecotec (out of fixture)

Cruze / Sonic / Tracker-era engines are modern Ecotec-family discussions.
Not in the current synthetic catalog; treat look-alike seals/gaskets with care.
