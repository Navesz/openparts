# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in OpenPartLine a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Welcome questions from beginners and experienced technicians alike.
- Give actionable feedback and acknowledge uncertainty.
- Credit sources and prior work; never invent catalog data to win an argument.
- Respect privacy, intellectual-property rights, and physical safety.
- Focus disagreement on evidence, not the person.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized
attention, trolling, personal attacks, deliberate misinformation (including fake
OEM numbers), publishing private information, or sustained disruption.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository activity, community spaces, and when someone
officially represents OpenPartLine.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not include a conduct report in a public issue. During the single-maintainer
bootstrap period, use GitHub private vulnerability reporting and begin the
summary with `[Conduct]`. This is a temporary private transport, not a claim
that misconduct is a software vulnerability.

Maintainers may remove content, warn, restrict, or ban according to impact.
Retaliation is prohibited.
